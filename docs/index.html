faq

good questions

what is flitty?

an ai buddy that lives next to your cursor on windows. hold the hotkey, ask anything about what's on your screen, and it answers out loud and points at the exact spot.

is it free?

flitty is free and open source. it uses your own openai api key, so you pay openai directly for what you use, usually a fraction of a cent per question.

is it watching my screen all the time?

no. it records only while you hold the hotkey and takes a screenshot only when you let go. recordings and screenshots are never saved, and there's no flitty server: they go straight from your pc to openai.

which apps does it work with?

anything on your screen. if you can see it, flitty can see it. no plugins needed.

does it work on mac?

windows comes first. a mac version is on the way.

windows says "windows protected your pc"

the installer isn't code-signed yet. click more info, then run anyway. you can read the source and build it yourself on github.

how do i get an openai key?

create one at platform.openai.com/api-keys, paste it into flitty's settings (click the tray icon), and press test key.
