How it works

Three seconds, no typing.

1

Hold and ask

Hold Ctrl Alt Space anywhere and ask like you'd ask a friend sitting next to you.

2

Let go

HeyFlitty looks at your screen and starts answering out loud, with every sentence captioned next to the cursor.

3

Follow the cursor

A little blue cursor flies to the exact control. On Windows it reads the real button positions, so it lands right on them.

Private by design

Your key. Your screen.
No middleman.

HeyFlitty has no servers and no accounts. It talks to OpenAI directly with your own key, and only when you ask.

⌨︎

Only when you hold the hotkey

It records while you hold the keys and takes one screenshot when you let go. Never in the background.

⇢

Straight to OpenAI

Your question, the screenshot and the names of the on-screen buttons go from your PC to OpenAI with your API key. You pay OpenAI directly, usually a fraction of a cent.

∅

Nothing saved

Recordings and screenshots are never written to disk. Your key stays in Windows Credential Manager.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is it free?

HeyFlitty is free and open source. It uses your own OpenAI API key, so you pay OpenAI directly for what you use, usually a fraction of a cent per question.

Is it watching my screen all the time?

No. It records only while you hold the hotkey and takes a screenshot only when you let go. Nothing is saved, and there's no HeyFlitty server.

Which apps does it work with?

Anything on your screen. On Windows it also reads the names and positions of buttons and menus, so pointing is exact in most apps.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes, from source for now. Windows is the focus; a signed Mac download is coming.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

The installer isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. You can read the source and build it yourself.

How do I get an OpenAI key?

Create one at platform.openai.com/api-keys, paste it into HeyFlitty's settings (click the tray icon) and press Test key.
