# Flitty — design spec

Concept: a firefly of light that finds the button for you. Dark, dusky frames for the open and close where the comet-orb glows brightest; the cream, editorial world of the website for the feature beats.

## Palette
- ink (dark stage bg): #141210 → dusk gradient to #1d2240 at the top
- cream (light stage bg): #f8f4ec, card #fffdf9, line #e4ddd0
- text on cream: #141210, muted #6f6a62
- text on ink: #f3efe7, muted #a39d93
- marker: #ffe066 (highlight swipe behind key words only)
- comet: core #ffffff, halo #9cc2ff, glow #3884ff (the only blue)

## Type
- Display: Instrument Serif (400, italic for the highlighted phrase), 120–180px headlines
- UI and labels: Geist 400/500/600, 24–34px; keycaps Geist 600

## Signature move
The comet orb: a white-hot core with a blue halo, trailing a soft tapered tail of fading light sampled from its own path. It enters every frame that has a target and always lands on it, then rests as Flitty's small blue pointer with a label.

## Motion
Calm-confident: ease-out entrances 0.4–0.7s, one hero move per frame, comet flights 0.9–1.4s on curved paths with ease-in-out. Film grain at low opacity across all frames.
