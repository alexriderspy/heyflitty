## Intent

A ~30s feature-tour marketing video for Flitty's landing page hero. Built as polished motion graphics. Flitty's cursor must move like a glowing comet / firefly orb with a soft light trail (the user's reference: a glowing ball of light flying with a comet tail across a dusk scene).

## Assets

- assets/logo.svg, assets/logo-white.svg — the Flitty flight-path mark; open and close.
- assets/real-demo.mp4 — real Windows 11 recording of Flitty pointing at GitHub's Issues tab; a short proof beat.

## Customizations

- Music bed only, no voiceover (the site autoplays muted, so on-screen type carries the words).
- Signature move: the comet-orb cursor with a soft light trail.
