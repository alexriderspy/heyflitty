# Flitty

Your AI screen buddy that shows you exactly where to click.

Hold a hotkey, ask out loud, and Flitty looks at your screen, answers in a voice, and flies a little cursor to the button you need. Open source, on macOS and Windows, with the AI provider of your choice.

> Status: early development. Not ready for use yet.
